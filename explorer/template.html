Fly / Experiment

Anatomy

216 selected, published MaleCNS neuron skeletons and 18 simplified neuropil surfaces in one specimen’s coordinate frame. Display units are micrometres. The brain view crops descending extensions; the source snapshot retains all skeleton nodes and edges. Ten right-side representatives per modeled population are accompanied by separately measured left-side and context neurons.

Connections

Eight right-side cell-type populations, represented by a schematic graph. Link thickness encodes symmetrized contacts. Nodes are populations, not individual neurons. This layout is independent of anatomical position.

Interference

Classical diffusion and a quantum walk share a scaled graph Laplacian. Continuous time evolution is evaluated directly from its eigensystem. Colored traces are quantum; pale traces are classical. Trace height shows probability, with each row scaled to its own maximum across the two models so low-probability populations remain visible. Phase mode uses the same row scaling and shows complex amplitude magnitude and relative phase around the time axis. Dim sections show future evolution; bright sections show elapsed evolution. Disconnections retain the intact time scale. Model time is dimensionless.

Comparison

Saved results from four preselected circuits on ibm_quebec, 1024 shots each, compared with ideal probabilities. No device phases are inferred. The initial-state control contains one off-source shot. Device noise, sampling error and unknown systematics remain in these frequencies.

Structure and small quantum circuits—not neuronal firing, fly behavior, biological quantum processing, or evidence of a speedup.
