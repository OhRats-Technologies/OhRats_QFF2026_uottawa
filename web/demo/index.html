Decisions, not reflexes.

Every front grows active fires. Crews subtract pressure for two fronts; smoke delays new dispatches to three. Water reduces pressure now. Toy pressure and this fire’s next-front reserve loss are visible. Fuel and exposure are in the readout description. Hover or focus a response to see its reserve and supplies after the current front.

Pressure, reserve and supplies are game units. Pressure ≤0.16 means containment; reserve ≤40 ends the season. Upgrades arrive after fronts 4 and 8. Map checkmarks show containment; corner numbers count fronts until a crew returns. There is no real-time clock.

What is real?

The Ontario boundary, 2021 land-cover raster and historical fire-position seeds. Dated canopy, recovery, water and 2026 fuel images come from official Canadian map services. They are context images, not validated game predictors. Grey cover pixels mean no mapped woodland class, not no vegetation.

Where is the quantum part?

The sphere illustrates angle encoding and single-qubit noise. The feature bench uses the project’s saved relevance/redundancy coefficients. Its diagonal projected matrix can be solved by taking the lowest sampled cost; classical enumeration of all 210 four-feature subsets is also trivial. Neither instrument boosts your crews.

Our research used a fidelity quantum kernel with support-vector regression for annual mean hectares per recorded Ontario fire. No tested final model beat the training-mean baseline. This game is a separate teaching experience.
