# Art and audio

`forest.png` is an original built-in ImageGen decorative environment, generated October 6, 2026. It depicts a fictional forest/lake, not mapped geography. The workbench map is the existing audited Ontario raster and 2021 point overlay; image generation is not used for geographic data.

Prompt: “Create an original pixel-art game environment asset: a cinematic Ontario conifer forest at dusk, a small distant wildfire glowing amber beyond a dark pine ridge and a lake reflecting its light. Landscape 16:9. Limited palette of petroleum teal, deep midnight green, burnt copper, amber and pale mint. Fine crafted 1990s simulation-game pixel art: individually articulated pine silhouettes, broken rocky shore, small rising embers, layered mist, fine dithering, delicate smoke. Dense visual richness, calm atmosphere, modest fire rather than disaster spectacle. No text, no letters, no UI, no borders, no logos, no people. Flat image meant to sit inside an in-game CRT. Crisp pixels and carefully composed silhouettes, no smooth 3D render.”

Frames, metal grain, bevels, screws, vents, pipes, phosphor scans, bitmap title and cable relays are original procedural canvas art. No concept screenshot is used as a clickable interface. No external fonts or rendering framework are required.

**Ember Relay** is an original procedural Web Audio theme: D Dorian, 78 BPM, 16-step plucked melody with a four-part bass cycle. Patching, running and completing a contract have related pitched cues. Music starts only through a user gesture, suspends while hidden, and can be muted. There is no autoplay or third-party audio download.

**Beatrice** is an original code-drawn pixel-art firefighter beaver (`beatrice.js`): copper helmet, reflective jacket, broad crosshatched tail, teeth and clipboard, with occasional blinking. She is a fictional Fireline character; no agency logo or affiliation is claimed. Her lessons and controls render on the same canvas as the game.

The guide now uses the short name **Betty**, with small speech bubbles attached to highlighted workbench controls. The original Beatrice sprite is retained, now with code-driven idle motion, a hop on Next and a drip torch on fire-related steps.

**Pixel Firefront (Relaxed Cover)** is an original recorded cover track added to `web/demo/assets/music/Pixel Firefront (Relaxed Cover).wav` (33 MB WAV audio asset), available for Fireline workbench and presentation background audio integration alongside the procedural Web Audio theme.
