Materials science · four qubits

SpinWeave: can a tiny magnet prove it is entangled?

Alternating strong and weak bonds change the ground state of a four-spin Heisenberg ring. Its fully separable energy bound is −1. An energy below that bound detects entanglement under the stated measurement assumptions.

SpinTherm · exact classical thermal-state analysis

Turn up the heat. Watch the witness disappear.

An energy witness can stop detecting entanglement without proving the state is separable. The answer also depends on which cut separates the spins.

At maximum dimerization the two pairs are independent: there is no entanglement across the pair/pair cut, even at zero temperature. Each pair loses its own entanglement at T = 2/ln(3). This is a finite four-spin crossover, not a phase transition. Gibbs states here are computed classically, not prepared on IBM.

Sustainability · four qubits

GridGuard: move the heavy jobs toward renewables.

Exactly two jobs must run late. Each slot has capacity 4. Early/late renewable supplies are 1/3; carbon factors are 0.55/0.18. All numbers are illustrative proxy units.

Rule check

The exact optimum and a simple greedy rule both give 0.73. Quantum optimization is a controlled comparison, not needed to solve four jobs.

Preparation matters as much as the mixer.

The paired start was cheap but weak. A uniform balanced Dicke start reaches about 99.3% optimal probability in the ideal p=2 simulation, at greater circuit cost.

XY exchanges preserve the number of late jobs ideally. Noise can break the constraint; report feasibility and optimality separately.

Hardware status

Three jobs, fourteen circuits, 1024 shots per circuit, and 300-second maximum execution allowance per job. A queued job is not a hardware result. Calibration forecasts below are local simulations.

Compare FlyWalk counts with the earlier calibration forecast

The forecast was sampled at 8192 shots. We treat those empirical frequencies as fixed probabilities and simulate 4000 new samples at the hardware budget of 1024 shots. The envelope omits uncertainty in the estimated forecast, calibration and drift. This exploratory diagnostic was chosen after counts returned; it is not a hardware confidence interval or a p-value.
