# Judge guide

**Question:** can quantum kernels improve retrospective annual Ontario wildfire-size estimation under a matched budget?

**Answer:** RBF leads the original matched chronological development comparison; none of eleven main models beats the training mean on six reused years. The useful quantum finding is a measured encoding-scale/conditioning tradeoff, not predictive advantage.

The later [real-device mitigation comparison](IBM_PIPELINE_MITIGATION.md) and [10/16/20-candidate hardware study](SELECTOR_HARDWARE.md) add actual counts, SQD subsets and fixed measured-kernel regressors. Read them as development diagnostics: ideal search quality, feasible hardware yield and prediction quality are different quantities. The original final result remains unchanged.

**What the hardware adds:** a measured diagnostic benchmark. More shots buy coverage, better QUBO energy need not help prediction, and matrix mitigation must be checked downstream. The [critique response](CRITIQUE_RESPONSE.md) includes small-sample sensitivity, measured exact-classical cost, classical-repair dependence and factual corrections; the [shot sweep](SHOT_SWEEP.md) reports both devices. These observations establish no quantum or device advantage.

## Read in this order

1. [README](../README.md): task, quantum contribution, data, results, costs and replay.
2. [Slides and browser presentation](../web/presentation/README.md): main talk and question appendix. Optional [Fireline](../web/demo/README.md) is a canvas engineering game: build a 2–10-input QSVR engine, inspect similarities and errors, then repair it. Its adaptive browser sandbox uses inspected development data; it does not change the scientific conclusions. The obsolete walkthrough is deleted.
3. [Report](REPORT.md): controlled comparisons, source assumptions, selection and negative results.
4. [Frozen final evidence](ANNUAL_FINAL.md): every annual error, spectra, recipes and public replay package.

The main story is annual estimation. [Earlier incident work](FINAL_EVALUATION.md), policy evolution and shot/tangent studies are supplemental; they do not supply independent annual evidence. Dataset construction and classical/QAOA/SQD selection are in the main talk. SQD is a diagonal-objective demonstration with no added optimization benefit. See the [independent raw-source audit](DATA_REVIEW.md) for annual totals, denominators and the large-fire explanation.

Later development evidence is separate from the frozen final comparison:

- [Comprehensive research findings](RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md): six new local studies and nine new IBM jobs, with source-free saved replay. Expanded tuning is unstable; better selection-objective sampling does not ensure better prediction. Hardware noise and all24 tuned-kernel repair outcomes remain visible. Original final results are unchanged.

- [Broader numerical forest panel](FOREST_CONTEXT.md): six structural/species families and dated memory proxies, 126 fixed fits including post-hoc graph-matched controls. Forest associations improve these fixed development models; reconstructed imagery prevents an as-of forecast claim.
- [Larger feature selection](SELECTOR_SCALING.md): 10/16/20 candidates, optimized QAOA and real addon SQD. At 20 candidates, p=2 finds the exact selection-objective minimum in 50/60 sampling trials versus 10/60 uniform, yet predicts worse with QSVR. Sampling quality is not predictive quality; exact enumeration is cheaper.
- [Three real IBM devices](IBM_PIPELINE_MITIGATION.md): six jobs / 75 charged QPU seconds, raw counts and mixed mitigation outcomes. One acquisition pair per device is descriptive, not a hardware ranking.

The earlier height-only pilot and [46-page catalogue audit](FIRE_FEATURE_SPACE.md) are retained as provenance. None of these followups retunes or replaces final annual predictions, and game instruments are educational.

## What is measured

31 annual training observations (1988–2018), six reused evaluation years (2019–2024); same-year climate, local analytic simulation. QSVR's support-vector optimizer is classical. No predictive accuracy claim follows from the later target-free bandwidth diagnostic. No hardware or quantum advantage is claimed.
